A managed marketplace service is an outsourced team that runs a brand's marketplace channels, such as Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop, day to day. The provider takes over account setup and health, catalog and listing optimization, advertising, pricing, inventory planning, brand protection, and marketplace customer service. The brand keeps ownership of its products, pricing strategy, and account.
