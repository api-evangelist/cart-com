Amazon listing optimization is the ongoing work of improving a product detail page so it ranks higher in Amazon search and converts more of the shoppers who see it. It covers the title, bullet points, images and video, A+ Content, backend search terms, and the product attributes Amazon uses to categorize and recommend items. Done well, it raises organic visibility, lowers the cost of advertising by improving conversion, and reduces returns by setting accurate expectations before purchase.
