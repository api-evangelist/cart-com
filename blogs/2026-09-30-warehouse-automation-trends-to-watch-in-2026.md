The warehouse automation market has experienced explosive growth , expanding from USD 29.91 billion in 2025 to a projected USD 63.36 billion by 2030. This 16.2% compound annual growth rate reflects urgent business needs: 80% of warehouses worldwide remain manually operated, while e-commerce volumes demand faster, more accurate fulfillment.
