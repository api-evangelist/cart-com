Short-term demand forecasting predicts how much of each product you will sell over the next few days to roughly three months, so you can decide what to reorder, where to position inventory, and how much warehouse labor to schedule. It differs from long-range planning because the decisions it drives are immediate and hard to reverse once a reorder window closes. For most growing brands, the right approach is not the most sophisticated model available.
